Do tego promptu dołączono odpowiedni opis z sekcji poniżej; każdy awatar powstał w osobnym wywołaniu narzędzia. Parametr `transparent_background` miał wartość `true`.

```text
Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: ONE premium illustrated profile avatar for an adult Polish camping and caravan travel app.
Style: warm hand-painted watercolor portrait, delicate natural paper-pigment texture within the painted subject, rounded readable shapes, lovingly painted realistic fur or feathers, friendly expressive eyes, restrained storybook quality for grown-up nature lovers. Harmonious pastel sage, teal, warm cream and ochre palette with a small sun-yellow accent. This belongs in a coherent collection of woodland travel companions. Not a photo, emoji, flat vector or 3D render.
Composition: square 1024x1024 canvas, one centered animal adventurer seen from chest up in a gentle front three-quarter portrait, face large and immediately recognizable at 56px. One simple travel accessory that reveals its personality. Entire ears, horns, hat and accessory visible, 14 percent transparent safe space around the subject for a circular crop. Friendly, relaxed and welcoming expression, never aggressive. Clean isolated cutout with genuinely transparent alpha background; no sky, landscape, enclosing circle, badge border, text, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, drop shadow or extra characters.
Subject:
```
