# Ilustracje strony powitalnej

Dwie ilustracje do CTA przed logowaniem. Lokalnie dołączone do aplikacji, z przezroczystym tłem PNG; nie wymagają połączenia z usługą obrazów.

Wygenerowane 23.10.2026 przez wbudowane narzędzie `imagegen` (bez CLI/API). Prompty poniżej są finalnymi promptami użytymi do generowania.

## `join-the-crew.png`

Plik: `assets/images/welcome/join-the-crew.png`.

```text
Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: a single transparent PNG illustration for the registration CTA card of the Polish camping travel app RazemGo. This is a finished app asset, not a UI mockup.
Primary request: create a NEW friendly watercolor camping illustration: two smiling adult travelers with small backpacks greeting each other beside a compact cream-and-muted-teal caravan, with a mustard canvas camping pennant and a few soft sage leaves. The theme is joining a welcoming community of travelers.
Style/medium: refined hand-painted watercolor and gouache, delicate paper grain inside the painted objects, soft organic outlines, warm natural light. Match a wholesome illustrated European camping travel journal, with convincing human anatomy and readable simple silhouettes at 112 pixels.
Composition/framing: square, centered compact composition occupying roughly 85% of canvas, two full-body adults and caravan forming one cohesive vignette, a small soft grass base; no scenery extending beyond the subject. Avoid miniature clutter. All subjects completely inside frame.
Color palette: muted sea blue and teal, sage and forest greens, warm cream, a little golden orange, soft slate shadows. Cheerful, calm, modern.
Constraints: genuinely transparent background, preserve clean alpha and softly painted edges; no white rectangle, no sky rectangle, no badge or circular border, no text, letters, logos, watermark, UI or buttons. Render one illustration only.
```

## `back-to-camp.png`

Plik: `assets/images/welcome/back-to-camp.png`.

```text
Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: a single transparent PNG illustration for the sign-in CTA card of the Polish camping travel app RazemGo. This is a finished app asset, not a UI mockup.
Primary request: create a NEW welcoming watercolor camping illustration symbolizing returning to your travel plans: a beautiful compact vintage muted-teal campervan with cream roof and its side door open, a warm mustard camping lantern glowing gently by the doorway, one cozy folding camping chair and a neatly folded travel map with a tiny compass in the foreground. No people. The campervan is the main easily recognizable subject.
Style/medium: refined hand-painted watercolor and gouache, delicate paper grain inside painted objects, soft organic outlines, warm natural light. Match a wholesome illustrated European camping travel journal and the companion registration illustration. Simple bold silhouette, readable at 112 pixels.
Composition/framing: square, centered compact three-quarter view vignette occupying roughly 85% of canvas, soft small grass base and a few sage leaves, all objects comfortably inside the canvas. Do not add a full landscape or miniature clutter.
Color palette: muted sea blue and teal, sage and forest greens, warm cream, little golden orange, soft slate shadows. Cheerful, calm, modern.
Constraints: genuinely transparent background with clean alpha and softly painted edges; no white rectangle, no sky rectangle, no badge or circular border, no text, letters, logos, watermark, UI or buttons. Render one illustration only.
```
